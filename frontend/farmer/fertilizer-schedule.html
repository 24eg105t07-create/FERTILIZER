Seasonal Fertilizer Application Schedule

6-Stage Nutrient Timeline

Follow this stage-wise application calendar to deliver nutrients precisely when plant roots can absorb them.

Standard Active Crop: Wheat / Field Crops
Stage 1: PRE-SOWING (Land Prep) Organic Manure & Humus
Focus Nutrient: Soil Organic Carbon & Biology
Timing: 15-20 days before sowing
Application Method: Deep incorporation via disc plough
Status: Completed

Incorporate 4-5 tonnes/acre of well-decomposed Farmyard Manure (FYM) or 1.5 tonnes/acre vermicompost to buffer soil moisture and support soil mycorrhiza.

Stage 2: BASAL (At Sowing / Day 0) Compound Starter (DAP / Complex) + Micronutrient
Focus Nutrient: Phosphorus + Starter N + Zinc
Timing: At sowing time
Application Method: Band placement 4-5 cm below seed
Status: Scheduled

Phosphorus is non-mobile in soil. Drill Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP) or SSP below seed depth so emerging radical roots immediately access phosphate for root elongation.

Stage 3: VEGETATIVE (Crown Root / Active Tillering) Nitrogen (Neem Coated Urea)
Focus Nutrient: Nitrogen (1st Split Top Dress)
Timing: 20-25 days after sowing (DAS)
Application Method: Broadcast followed by light irrigation
Status: Upcoming

Apply 1st split of Neem Coated Urea under field-capacity moisture to boost tiller count and photosynthetic leaf area.

Stage 4: FLOWERING (Jointing to Panicle / Tassel Initiation) Nitrogen & Potash (Urea + MOP)
Focus Nutrient: Nitrogen (2nd Split) + Potassium
Timing: 45-55 days after sowing (DAS)
Application Method: Surface broadcasting in damp soil
Status: Upcoming

Crucial phase determining grain/spikelet count. Avoid late nitrogen spikes which cause crop lodging or succulent stems susceptible to fungal rusts.

Stage 5: FRUIT / GRAIN DEVELOPMENT (Milking to Dough) Water Soluble Potassium & Micronutrients
Focus Nutrient: Potassium Translocation & Boron
Timing: 70-80 days after sowing
Application Method: Foliar spray during cool evening hours
Status: Upcoming

Demo guidance: 1% foliar spray of Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) or SOP (0:0:50) prevents premature flag leaf drying and maximizes 1000-grain weight.

Stage 6: HARVEST & RESIDUE MANAGEMENT Crop Stubble Incorporation
Focus: Soil Nutrient Recycling & Carbon Audit
Timing: Post-harvest
Method: In-situ shredding and soil incorporation
Status: Post-Season

Do NOT burn crop residue. Incorporating straw returns up to 80% of potassium and 40% of phosphorus back to the soil bank for subsequent crop cycles.

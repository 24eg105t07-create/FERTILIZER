Understanding What's in Your Soil

A healthy crop requires balanced nutrition across primary macronutrients, secondary minerals, organic carbon, and trace micronutrients. AgriWise analyzes all available parameters to formulate an explainable 4R fertilizer schedule.

Primary Nutrients (N - P - K)
The core drivers of plant biomass and harvest yield
  • Nitrogen (N): Essential for chlorophyll, protein synthesis, and vigorous vegetative tillering. Optimal: 280 - 560 kg/ha. Deficient soils require split applications to avoid leaching.
  • Phosphorus (P): Critical for early root architecture, energy transfer (ATP), and flower initiation. Optimal: 23 - 56 kg/ha. Applied basally below seed furrow for maximum uptake.
  • Potassium (K): Regulates water stomatal closure, disease resistance, and carbohydrate grain filling. Optimal: 145 - 335 kg/ha. Protects crops against moisture stress and lodging.
Soil Chemistry (pH & EC)
Controls nutrient solubility and microbial vitality
  • Soil Reaction (pH): Optimal neutral range is 6.5 - 7.5. Acidic soils (< 6.5) lock phosphorus; alkaline soils (> 7.8) precipitate zinc and iron, causing leaf chlorosis.
  • Electrical Conductivity (EC): Measures total soluble salts. Safe: < 1.0 dS/m. Elevated levels (> 2.0 dS/m) cause osmotic stress and root tip burning.
  • Organic Carbon (%): The biological engine of soil cation exchange. Target: > 0.75%. Low carbon soils require compost, FYM, or green manuring to sustain nutrient retention.
Secondary Nutrients (S - Ca - Mg)
Vital structural elements and enzyme activators
  • Sulfur (S): Required for oil synthesis in mustard, groundnut, soybean, and essential amino acids. Target: 10 - 20 ppm. Supplied via SSP or gypsum.
  • Calcium (Ca): Strengthens cell walls; prevents blossom-end rot in vegetables and fruit cracking.
  • Magnesium (Mg): The central atom in every chlorophyll molecule; drives photosynthesis.
Essential Micronutrients (Zn, Fe, B)
Micro quantities with profound impact on harvest quality
  • Zinc (Zn): Critical for auxin plant hormones. Deficient in 40%+ of Asian agricultural soils. Critical threshold: 0.60 ppm. Corrected with Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO4).
  • Iron (Fe) & Boron (B): Iron activates respiration; Boron ensures pollen viability and fruit setting.
  • Manganese (Mn) & Copper (Cu): Catalyze nitrogen metabolism and fungal defense.

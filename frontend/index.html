PRECISION NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT

Why Traditional Fertilizer Application Fails

Blindly adding more fertilizer degrades soil biology, leaches into groundwater, and burns farmer profits without increasing yields.

Prevent Nutrient Imbalance

Continuous high nitrogen application without adequate phosphorus or potash induces vegetative lodging, increases pest infestation, and depletes organic carbon.

Agronomist Review & Safety

No automated black-box recommendations. Every AI rule-generated recommendation is scrutinized and approved by certified agronomists before dispatch to farmers.

6-Stage Application Schedule

Transform complex fertilizer charts into actionable stages: Pre-sowing, Basal, Vegetative Tillering, Flowering, Grain Development, and Residue Incorporation.

Transparent Explainability

Every recommendation clearly states the "WHY": which soil parameter triggered the advisory, the crop growth stage demand, and specific field topography context.

Cost & Runoff Reduction

Avoid unnecessary expenditures on nutrients already abundant in your soil. Mitigate nitrogen runoff and phosphate fixation with customized placement techniques.

Multi-Region Architecture

Engineered for diverse agro-climatic zones, state soil classifications (Alluvial, Black, Red, Sandy Loam), irrigation infrastructure, and multi-crop rotations.

SCALABLE ARCHITECTURE

Engineered for Every State, Soil, and Farming System

AgriWise is built with configurable agronomic thresholds. Whether you are cultivating Wheat in Punjab or Haryana, Rice in Telangana or West Bengal, Cotton in Gujarat or Maharashtra, or Vegetables under Drip Fertigation, the system adapts dynamically to local soil chemistry.

Major Soil Types

Alluvial, Black Vertisols, Red Laterite, Sandy Loam, Peaty.

Water Sources

Canal Network, Tubewell, Drip Fertigation, Rainfed Basin.
